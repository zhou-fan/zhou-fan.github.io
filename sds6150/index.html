S&DS 6150: Introduction to random matrix theory and applications

Zhou Fan, Yale University, Fall 2026

Description

An introduction to random matrix theory. Wigner matrices, sample covariance matrices, spiked models. Applications to principal components analysis, covariance estimation, ridge regression, analyses of sparse random graphs. Methods for non-invariant models that commonly arise in applications: moment method, concentration of measure, resolvents and Stieltjes transforms, free probability, Lindeberg exchange.

Prerequisites: Measure-theoretic probability, basic linear algebra

Your report should contain a technical summary of the main results/ideas of the paper(s) and, in addition, do at least one of the following:

  • Provide an in-depth discussion/development of a central argument or technique of the paper, for example by illustrating the argument in a simpler special case/application or extending the argument to a variant of the studied model.
  • Implement and test a numerical algorithm or method that is proposed in the paper.
  • Integrate and explain the connections between two or more papers that pertain to a similar topic or problem.
