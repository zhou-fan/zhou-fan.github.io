Zhou Fan

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Statistics and Data Science at Yale University. My research interests lie at the intersection of mathematical statistics, probability theory, and computational algorithms. I am particularly interested in inferential problems that arise in scientific applications, including statistical genetics and computational biology.
